About JCDA

WHY WE EXIST

JCDA was created as a community organization to bring people together and support local development. Our association works with residents, young people and community groups in an effort to have a positive impact on society.

Our Vision

We want communities where people have opportunities to learn, participate and help one another.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help communities grow by providing useful programmes, supporting young people and encouraging people to work together.

Our Values

  • Teamwork
  • Respect
  • Honesty
  • Community service
  • Education
